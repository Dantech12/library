Full refunds

We refund the full amount if:

  • you were charged twice or charged by mistake;
  • you ask within 7 days of your first payment, before setup of your school's library is finished; or
  • we cannot deliver the setup we promised within 14 days of your payment.

After that

Once a period has started and your library is set up, payments for that period are not refundable. You can cancel at any time to stop future payments, and you keep the paid services until the end of the period you paid for.
