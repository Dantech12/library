Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the {{BUSINESS_NAME}} collects and how we use it. We collect as little as possible.

Students using the library

  • Each student's login is created by their school. For it we store only the student's name, class (if the school adds one), username, a scrambled (hashed) form of the password, and the date they last signed in. We never store passwords in readable form.
  • The school decides who gets a login and can remove a student at any time. Removing a student deletes their login.
  • Signing in uses one cookie that keeps the student signed in on that device. It is not used for anything else.
  • Reading progress, favourites and settings are saved only on the student's own device (in the browser's storage). They are never sent to us.
  • When a student uses the Encyclopedia search, the search words are sent to Wikipedia to fetch the article. Wikipedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • The library has no advertising and no tracking or analytics.

Schools that buy a plan

When you buy a plan, we collect your school or organization name, your name, email address and phone number. We use them to set up your library, send receipts and reminders, and provide support. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.

Payments

Payments are handled by Paystack. You enter your card or Mobile Money details on Paystack's secure checkout, and we never see or store them. Paystack tells us whether a payment succeeded, the amount, and the email used. Paystack's privacy policy applies to the information you give them.

How long we keep information

We keep payment records for as long as the law requires for accounting, and contact details for as long as you are a customer and up to 12 months after. You can ask us to delete your contact details at any time, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
