FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it legal to share these books with students?

Yes. Every book in the library is in the public domain and comes from Project Gutenberg, so it is free to read, copy and share. Encyclopedia articles come from Wikipedia under an open license.

Do students need internet?

Not for reading. All books are stored with the library, so they work offline. Only the encyclopedia search and the "More Online" links need a connection.

Which devices does it work on?

Any device with a modern web browser: Android tablets and phones, iPads, Chromebooks, Windows and Mac laptops. Nothing needs to be installed.

How do students sign in?

Your school admin pastes in the class lists, and every student gets a username and an easy password like mango-4821. Students sign in with your school code, username and password. If someone forgets their password, the admin makes a new one in seconds. We only store each student's name and class. Reading progress stays on the device.

Can we add our own books or class readers?

Yes. Any of the 70,000+ free books on Project Gutenberg can be added. On the School and Network plans, we add up to 50 books each term for you on request.

What happens if we stop paying?

You keep the free Starter library forever. Hosting, branding and support stop at the end of the period you paid for.
